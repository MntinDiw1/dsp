Real-time typing-speed monitor. A piezo sensor on the laptop picks up the vibration of each keystroke; the signal is sampled by an Arduino (pyFirmata2, ~1 kHz) and cleaned in real time by a chain of IIR filters (high-pass, 50 Hz notch, band-pass) so individual keystrokes can be detected. The keystroke rate is smoothed with an IIR low-pass into words per minute (1 word = 5 characters) and shown on two 7-segment displays. Telling the spacebar apart from other keys (a true word count) is an extension if the first version works.
